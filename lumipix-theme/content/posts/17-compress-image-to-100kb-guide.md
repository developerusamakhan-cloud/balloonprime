100KB is the most common file size limit on the web. Exam boards, job portals, visa applications and profile uploads all use it. It is enough room for a clear portrait or a readable document, as long as the compression is done carefully.

## One-step method for every device

1. Open [compress image to 100KB](tool:compress-image-to-100kb).
2. Drop your photo, or tap **Choose files** on a phone.
3. Download the result.

It works the same on Android, iPhone, Windows and Mac because it runs in the browser. The photo stays on your device and you can process up to 20 images at once.

## What a 100KB photo looks like

At 100KB, a portrait can comfortably be 1000–1600 pixels wide with good JPG quality. That is sharp on any screen and more than any form needs. Most people cannot tell a well-compressed 100KB portrait from the multi-megabyte original when viewed at normal size.

## Why the target-size approach works better

Ordinary compressors make you guess a quality percentage, save, check the size, and try again. A target-size compressor reverses that: you give it the limit, and it tests quality levels automatically to find the highest one that fits. If quality alone is not enough, it reduces dimensions gradually. You get the best image for the limit in one go.

## Doing it without a website

**Windows:** in Paint, use Resize to about 30–40% and save as JPEG, then check the file size in Properties. Repeat if needed.

**Mac:** in Preview, choose File → Export, select JPEG and move the Quality slider until the file size shown is under 100KB.

**iPhone and Android:** there is no built-in KB control, so a browser tool is the quickest option.

These methods work, but take several attempts. Our guide to [reducing image size in KB](post:how-to-reduce-image-size-in-kb) covers them in more detail.

## Step by step on a computer

1. Open [compress image to 100KB](tool:compress-image-to-100kb).
2. Drag one or more photos from File Explorer or Finder onto the drop area.
3. Wait until each file shows **Fits target**.
4. Click **Download** for one file, or **Download all** for the batch.
5. Find the files in your Downloads folder, with "-compressed" in the name.

## Choosing quality vs dimensions

At 100KB you have room for choices:

- **Portraits for forms:** let the tool decide. It keeps the face sharp and reduces dimensions only if needed.
- **Documents:** set a **Maximum width** of about 1600 pixels under **Advanced options**, so text stays readable rather than very large but blurry.
- **Website images:** decide the width you need (for example 1200 px) and set it as the maximum width.

## When the form also has a minimum

Some portals ask for a file "between 50KB and 100KB". The compressor never goes over your target, but it also stops once the photo is at high quality, so a simple photo can come out well under the limit. If that happens, raise the **Maximum width** under **Advanced options** (or leave it empty) so more pixels are kept, or start from a larger original.

## Checklist before you submit

- The file ends in **.jpg** (or the format the form asks for).
- The size shown is **under 100KB**.
- The face or text is **clear at 100% zoom**.
- You are uploading the **compressed copy**, not the original.
- You have kept the original in case you need a different size later.

## Key takeaways

- **100KB is enough** for a sharp portrait or a readable single-page document.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I compress a JPG to 100KB?

Use a target-size compressor set to 100KB. It adjusts quality and, if necessary, dimensions until the JPG fits.

### Can I compress a 5MB photo to 100KB?

Yes. A 5MB phone photo can be reduced to 100KB with good visible quality by lowering both the dimensions and the JPG quality.

### Is 100KB good quality for a passport photo upload?

Yes, 100KB is plenty for a clear passport-style photo on an online form. Check the form's own limits and dimensions.

### What is the best width for a 100KB portrait?

Usually 1000 to 1600 pixels, depending on the background. The compressor picks the largest size that still looks sharp.

### Can I compress multiple photos to 100KB at once?

Yes. Drop up to 20 photos together. Each is compressed to the target, and Download all saves them in one step.

### Does compressing to 100KB remove the photo's location data?

When the photo is re-encoded, hidden details such as GPS location and camera settings are not copied to the new file. If the original is already under the limit and in the right format, it is kept unchanged, including that data.
