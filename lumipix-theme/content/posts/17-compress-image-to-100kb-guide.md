100KB is probably the most common file size limit on the web. Exam boards, job portals, visa applications and profile uploads all use it. It's enough room for a clear portrait or a readable document, as long as the compression is done with a bit of care. Here's how to compress an image to 100KB on any device.

## The one-step way, on any device

1. Open the page to [compress image to 100KB](tool:compress-image-to-100kb).
2. Drop your photo in, or tap **Choose files** on a phone.
3. Download the result.

It works the same on Android, iPhone, Windows and Mac because it runs in your browser. The photo stays on your device, and you can do up to 20 images at once.

## What a 100KB photo looks like

At 100KB, a portrait can comfortably be 1000 to 1600 pixels wide at good JPG quality. That's sharp on any screen and more than any form needs. Most people can't tell a well compressed 100KB portrait from the multi-megabyte original at normal viewing size.

## Why a target size works better

Ordinary compressors make you guess a quality percentage, save, check the size, and try again. A target size compressor flips that around. You give it the limit, and it tries quality levels for you to find the highest one that fits. If quality alone isn't enough, it brings the dimensions down a little at a time. You get the best image for the limit in one go.

## Doing it without a website

**Windows.** In Paint, use Resize to about 30 to 40% and save as JPEG, then check the size in Properties. Repeat if you need to.

**Mac.** In Preview, choose File, Export, select JPEG and move the Quality slider until the size shown is under 100KB.

**iPhone and Android.** There's no built-in KB setting, so a browser tool is the quickest way.

These work, but they take a few attempts. Our guide on [how to reduce image size in KB](post:how-to-reduce-image-size-in-kb) goes through them in more detail.

## On a computer, step by step

1. Open the 100KB page.
2. Drag one or more photos from File Explorer or Finder onto the drop area.
3. Wait until each one says **Fits target**.
4. Click **Download** for one file, or **Download all** for the batch.
5. Find them in your Downloads folder with "-compressed" in the name.

If the form also needs specific dimensions, set them first in the [image resizer](tool:image-resizer), then compress.

## Quality or dimensions?

At 100KB you've got some room to choose:

- **Portraits for forms.** Let the tool decide. It keeps the face sharp and only reduces the dimensions if it has to.
- **Documents.** Set a **Maximum width** of about 1600 pixels under **Advanced options**, so text stays readable instead of huge but blurry.
- **Website images.** Decide the width you need, say 1200 px, and set it as the maximum width.

## When there's a minimum too

Some portals ask for a file "between 50KB and 100KB". The compressor never goes over your target, but it also stops once the photo is at high quality, so a simple photo can come in well under. If that happens, raise the **Maximum width** under **Advanced options** (or leave it empty) so more pixels are kept, or start from a bigger original.

## Uploading on a phone

On Android, the compressed file goes into **Downloads**. On iPhone, it's in the **Files** app. When the form shows the upload button, choose **Browse** or **Files** rather than your photo gallery. Otherwise you might pick the original by mistake. For a full walkthrough of an application, see [how to compress a photo for online application forms](post:compress-photo-for-online-forms).

## Checklist before you submit

- The file ends in **.jpg**, or whatever format the form asks for.
- The size shown is **under 100KB**.
- The face or text is **clear at 100% zoom**.
- You're uploading the **compressed copy**, not the original.
- You've kept the original in case you need a different size later.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I compress a JPG to 100KB?

Use a target size compressor set to 100KB. It adjusts the quality and, if it needs to, the dimensions until the JPG fits.

### Can I compress a 5MB photo to 100KB?

Yes. A 5MB phone photo can come down to 100KB and still look good, by lowering both the dimensions and the JPG quality.

### Is 100KB good quality for a passport photo upload?

Yes, 100KB is plenty for a clear passport-style photo on an online form. Check the form's own limits and dimensions too.

### What is the best width for a 100KB portrait?

Usually 1000 to 1600 pixels, depending on the background. The compressor picks the biggest size that still looks sharp.

### Can I compress multiple photos to 100KB at once?

Yes. Drop up to 20 photos together. Each one is compressed to the target, and Download all saves them in one step.

### Does compressing to 100KB remove the photo's location data?

When the photo is re-encoded, hidden details like GPS location and camera settings aren't copied to the new file. If the original is already under the limit and in the right format, it's kept as it is, including that data.
