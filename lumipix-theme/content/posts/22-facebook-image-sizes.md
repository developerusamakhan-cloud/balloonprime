Facebook displays images in many places, each with its own shape. Uploading at a sensible size avoids awkward crops and blurry text. Here are the dimensions most people need, with a reminder that Meta adjusts its layouts from time to time, so check its help pages before a big campaign.

## Facebook image sizes at a glance

| Placement | Recommended size | Aspect ratio |
|---|---|---|
| Feed post, square | 1080 × 1080 px | 1:1 |
| Feed post, portrait | 1080 × 1350 px | 4:5 |
| Link preview (shared link image) | 1200 × 630 px | 1.91:1 |
| Story | 1080 × 1920 px | 9:16 |
| Cover photo (page or profile) | 851 × 315 px or larger | about 2.7:1 |
| Profile picture | at least 320 × 320 px | 1:1, shown as a circle |
| Event cover | 1920 × 1005 px | about 1.91:1 |

## Feed posts

Square and 4:5 portrait images take up the most room in the feed on phones, where most people scroll. These are the same sizes Instagram uses, so one image can work for both. See our [Instagram image sizes guide](post:instagram-image-sizes) for more.

## Link previews

When you share a link, Facebook uses the page's Open Graph image, ideally 1200 × 630 pixels. Keep important text near the centre, because some placements crop the edges. Every Lumi Pix page and article includes a correctly sized share image for exactly this reason.

## Cover photos

Cover photos are cropped differently on desktop and mobile. Use an image wider than the minimum (for example 1640 × 624 for extra sharpness) and keep faces and text in the central area so nothing important is cut off on either device.

## Profile pictures

Profile pictures are shown as a circle. Centre your face, leave a little space around it, and upload a square image so you control the crop.

## How to resize for Facebook

1. Open the [Image Resizer](tool:image-resizer) and drop your image.
2. Turn off the aspect lock and type the size from the table, or use the square, portrait and story presets on the [Instagram resizer](tool:resize-image-for-instagram), which share the same sizes.
3. Choose **Fill** to crop to the frame, or **Fit** to keep the whole picture with a background colour.
4. Download as JPG for photos, or PNG for graphics with text.

## Tips for sharper Facebook images

- Upload at the recommended size, not much larger or smaller.
- Use PNG for graphics with small text; JPG for photos.
- Avoid putting large amounts of text on images used in ads.

For resizing to exact dimensions in general, see [how to resize a picture in pixels](post:resize-picture-in-pixels).

## Frequently asked questions

### What size should a Facebook post image be?

1080 × 1080 pixels for square posts or 1080 × 1350 for portrait posts work well in the feed.

### What is the best size for a Facebook link preview image?

1200 × 630 pixels, with key content near the centre.

### Why is my Facebook cover photo cropped?

Desktop and mobile show different parts of the cover. Use a large image and keep important content in the middle.
