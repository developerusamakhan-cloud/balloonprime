A transparent background turns an ordinary photo into something you can use anywhere: a product shot for your shop, a profile picture with a new colour behind you, a cut-out for a poster. You don't need expensive software for it. Here are five free ways to remove the background from an image, and when each one makes sense.

## Method 1: An AI background remover in your browser

For most photos, the quickest option is an AI tool that finds the subject for you.

1. Open the tool to [remove background for free online](tool:background-remover).
2. Drop your photo in.
3. Wait a few seconds. The first time, the AI model downloads once and your browser keeps it.
4. Choose a new background: transparent, white, black or any colour.
5. Download a full resolution PNG, or a JPG with a solid background.

The Lumi Pix remover runs the AI **on your device**, so the photo isn't uploaded, and the full-size download is free without an account.

**Best for:** portraits, people, pets and products with a clear subject.

## Method 2: Your iPhone or iPad

Recent versions of iOS can lift the subject out of a photo. Open the picture in Photos, press and hold on the subject until it glows, then choose **Copy** or **Share**. You can paste the cut-out into another app.

**Best for:** quick cut-outs for messages and notes. You don't get much control over the edges or the resolution, though.

## Method 3: Windows Paint

In recent updates, Paint on Windows 11 has a background removal button. Open the image, click **Remove background** in the toolbar, and save as PNG to keep the transparency.

**Best for:** Windows users who want something built in.

## Method 4: PowerPoint

Insert the picture, select it, then go to **Picture Format → Remove Background**. PowerPoint marks the areas it'll remove in purple. Use **Mark Areas to Keep** and **Mark Areas to Remove** to tidy it up, then right-click the picture and choose **Save as Picture** in PNG format.

**Best for:** presentations, when you're already working in Office.

## Method 5: Photoshop or GIMP

For the most control, use a full editor. In Photoshop, select the layer and use **Remove Background** in the contextual task bar or the Properties panel, then refine the mask. In GIMP, which is free, use the **Foreground Select** tool and clean up the edges by hand.

**Best for:** hair, fur, glass and tricky edges that need manual work.

## Getting better results from AI

AI tools are good, but the photo you start with makes a big difference.

- **Contrast helps.** A dark subject on a light background, or the other way round, gives cleaner edges than similar colours.
- **Keep the subject sharp.** Blurry edges confuse the model.
- **Watch out for things touching the subject.** A plant that seems to grow out of someone's head is hard for any tool to separate.
- **Use the biggest original.** More pixels give the model more edge detail to work with.

If the result is close but not quite right, try a slightly different crop of the same photo. The model sometimes does better when the subject fills more of the frame.

## Hair, fur and fine detail

Hair and fur are the hardest part of any cut-out, because individual strands mix with the background. For the best result:

1. Photograph the subject against a plain background that contrasts with the hair.
2. Run it through the AI remover and check the edges at 100% zoom.
3. If a few strands look rough, put the cut-out on a colour similar to the original background. Small flaws disappear.

For professional work like product catalogues or print, a manual touch-up in Photoshop or GIMP can still be worth it.

### How do I remove a background for free without losing quality?

Use a tool that saves at the original resolution as PNG. The Lumi Pix background remover gives you a full-size transparent PNG for free with no signup.

### Why does my transparent image show a white background?

It was probably saved as JPG, which can't store transparency. Save or download it as PNG instead.

### Can I replace the background with a colour?

Yes. Pick a colour before you download, or keep it transparent and put the cut-out on any background later.

### Can I remove a background on my phone?

### Does the background remover work offline?

Once the AI model has downloaded, your browser keeps it, so later images can be processed without an internet connection.
