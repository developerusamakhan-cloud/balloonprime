A transparent background turns an ordinary photo into something you can use anywhere: a product shot for a shop, a profile picture with a new colour behind it, or a cut-out for a poster. You do not need expensive software. Here are five free methods and when each one makes sense.

## Method 1: An AI background remover in your browser

The quickest option for most photos is an AI tool that detects the subject automatically.

1. Open the [Background Remover](tool:background-remover).
2. Drop your photo.
3. Wait a few seconds. The first time, the AI model downloads once and is then cached.
4. Choose a new background: transparent, white, black or any colour.
5. Download a full-resolution PNG (or JPG with a solid background).

The Lumi Pix remover runs the AI **on your device**, so the photo is never uploaded, and the full-size download is free without an account.

**Best for:** portraits, people, pets and products with a clear subject.

## Method 2: Your iPhone or iPad

Recent versions of iOS can lift the subject from a photo. Open the picture in Photos, press and hold on the subject until it glows, then choose **Copy** or **Share**. You can paste the cut-out into another app.

**Best for:** quick cut-outs for messages and notes. You have little control over edges or resolution.

## Method 3: Windows Paint

Paint in Windows 11 includes a background removal button in recent updates. Open the image, select **Remove background** in the toolbar, and save as PNG to keep transparency.

**Best for:** Windows users who want a built-in option.

## Method 4: PowerPoint

Insert the picture, select it, then go to **Picture Format → Remove Background**. PowerPoint marks areas to remove in purple. Use **Mark Areas to Keep** and **Mark Areas to Remove** to refine, then right-click the picture and choose **Save as Picture** in PNG format.

**Best for:** presentations, when you are already working in Office.

## Method 5: Photoshop or GIMP

For the most control, use a full editor. In Photoshop, select the layer and use **Remove Background** in the contextual task bar or the Properties panel, then refine the mask. In the free editor GIMP, use the **Foreground Select** tool and refine edges by hand.

**Best for:** hair, fur, glass and complex edges that need manual cleanup.

## Special case: logos, signatures and white backgrounds

If the background is already a single flat colour, such as a scanned signature or a logo on white, you do not need AI. The [white background remover](tool:remove-white-background) turns white, or any colour you click, transparent instantly and keeps edges smooth.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I remove a background for free without losing quality?

Use a tool that exports at the original resolution as PNG. The Lumi Pix Background Remover gives a full-size transparent PNG for free with no signup.

### Why does my transparent image show a white background?

It was probably saved as JPG, which cannot store transparency. Save or download as PNG instead.

### Can I replace the background with a colour?

Yes. Pick a colour before downloading, or keep it transparent and place the cut-out on any background later.
