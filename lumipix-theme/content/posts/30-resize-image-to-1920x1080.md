1920 × 1080, also called Full HD or 1080p, is the most common screen size in the world. It is the standard for desktop wallpapers, presentation slides, video frames and TV displays. Here is how to resize any image to it without stretching.

## Understand the shape first

1920 × 1080 has a **16:9** aspect ratio: wide and short. Most phone photos are 4:3, and portrait photos are tall. If you force a different shape into 16:9, it stretches. Instead, choose one of two approaches:

- **Fill:** the photo fills the whole 1920 × 1080 frame and the top and bottom (or sides) are cropped.
- **Fit:** the whole photo stays visible and the empty space is filled with a colour, like letterboxing.

## Step by step

1. Open the [Image Resizer](tool:image-resizer).
2. Tap the **Full HD 1920×1080** preset, or turn off the link icon and type 1920 and 1080.
3. Choose **Fill – crop edges** for wallpapers and thumbnails, or **Fit – add background** to keep the entire image.
4. For Fit, pick a background colour. Black suits presentations and screens; a colour from the photo looks more designed.
5. Download as JPG.

You can drop up to 20 photos at once, which is handy for a slideshow. Nothing is uploaded.

## Choosing between Fill and Fit

| Use | Best mode |
|---|---|
| Desktop wallpaper | Fill |
| Video thumbnail | Fill |
| Slideshow on a TV | Fit (black) |
| Presentation slide background | Fill |
| Portrait photo on a widescreen | Fit |

## What if my image is smaller than 1920×1080?

The resizer enlarges it, but enlarging cannot add detail, so a small image may look soft at full screen. If possible, start from a higher-resolution original. Most phone photos are larger than 1920 pixels wide, so this is rarely a problem.

All share the same 16:9 shape, so the same Fill or Fit choice applies. The resizer has an **HD 1280×720** preset too.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I resize a photo to 1920×1080 without stretching?

Turn off the aspect lock, enter 1920 × 1080 and choose Fill to crop or Fit to add a background, instead of Stretch.

### Is 1920×1080 the same as 1080p?

Yes. 1080p, Full HD and 1920 × 1080 all describe the same 16:9 resolution.

### What is the best format for a 1920×1080 wallpaper?

JPG for photos, at high quality. PNG for graphics with flat colours and text.
