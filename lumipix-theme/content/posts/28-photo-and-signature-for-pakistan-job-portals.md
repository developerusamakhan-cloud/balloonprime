Recruitment and testing portals in Pakistan want a photo and signature for PPSC, FPSC and NTS forms, and often scanned documents too. Every upload has rules on size and format, and a rejected file close to the deadline is the last thing you need. Use this checklist to get everything ready before you even open the form.

**Please note:** each organisation sets its own requirements, and they can change between advertisements. Always read the instructions for the specific post you're applying for. Lumi Pix is an independent tool and isn't connected to PPSC, FPSC, NTS or any government department.

## First: read the instructions

From the advertisement or the portal's help page, write down:

- the photo **file size limit** (in KB) and **format** (usually JPG)
- the photo **dimensions** or shape, if given
- the **background colour** for the photo
- the signature size limit and format
- which **documents** you need to upload, and their limits

## The photograph

1. Take a recent photo against a plain, light background, facing the camera, in even light.
2. Crop it to head and shoulders, or use the [passport size photo creator](tool:passport-size-photo) if a particular shape is required.
3. Compress it to the limit from the instructions. There are pages to [compress photo for PPSC](tool:compress-photo-for-ppsc), [compress photo for FPSC](tool:compress-photo-for-fpsc) and [compress photo for NTS](tool:compress-photo-for-nts) with quick size buttons, or use the main [image compressor](tool:compress-image).
4. Open the result and check your face is sharp.

## The signature

1. Sign with a dark pen on plain white paper.
2. Photograph it from straight above in good light and crop it tight.
3. [Resize signature online](tool:resize-signature) and pick the limit from the instructions.

There's more detail in our [signature resizing guide](post:resize-signature-for-online-form).

## Document scans

CNIC copies, degrees, transcripts and domicile certificates should be flat, well lit and fully readable. [Compress image to 200KB](tool:compress-image-to-200kb), or whatever the limit is, for each one. Our guide to [compressing document scans](post:compress-document-scan-to-200kb) walks through it. Make sure the numbers and dates can still be read.

## Doing it all on your phone

Lots of candidates apply from a phone. Here's a full routine that works in any mobile browser:

1. **Photo.** Ask someone to take your photo against a plain light wall with the back camera. Crop to head and shoulders in your gallery.
2. **Signature.** Sign on white paper, photograph it from above in daylight, and crop it tight.
3. **Documents.** Use your phone's document scanner (Notes on iPhone, Google Drive on Android) for each certificate and both sides of your CNIC.
4. Open the Lumi Pix tools you need and set each file to its limit.
5. Download each result and give it a clear name, like `photo.jpg`, `signature.jpg` and `cnic-front.jpg`.
6. Upload from the **Downloads** folder (Android) or the **Files** app (iPhone).

## What to look for in the instructions

Each organisation and advertisement sets its own rules. When you read them, note these down:

| Item | What to note |
|---|---|
| Photograph | Maximum KB, format, dimensions, background colour |
| Signature | Maximum KB, format, sometimes dimensions |
| CNIC, domicile, degrees | Maximum KB per file, format, colour or greyscale |
| Experience letters | Maximum KB, and whether several pages are allowed |
| Fee challan | Usually a scan or photo of the paid receipt |

If something isn't clear, the organisation's help desk or FAQ page has the final word.

## Photo do's and don'ts

**Do:**

- use a recent photo
- face the camera with a neutral expression
- wear something that stands out from the background
- keep it sharp and evenly lit

**Don't:**

- use a selfie taken at arm's length
- use beauty filters
- crop yourself out of a group photo
- upload a photo of a printed photo

## Final checklist before you upload

- Photo: right size limit, JPG, plain background, clear face
- Signature: right size limit, JPG, dark and readable
- Documents: readable, under the limit, right format
- Simple file names without special characters
- Copies of everything saved in one folder for next time

## If an upload fails

- **"File too large".** Choose a slightly smaller limit. Some portals count 1KB as 1,000 bytes.
- **"Invalid format".** Make sure the file ends in .jpg. iPhone photos may be HEIC, but Lumi Pix saves JPG automatically.
- **"Invalid dimensions".** Set the stated size in the [image resizer](tool:image-resizer), then compress again.
- **The portal is struggling near the deadline.** Busy portals get slow. Get your files ready early and try again later.

## Avoiding last-day stress

Portals are usually busiest right before the closing date. To stay out of trouble:

- Get all your files ready at least a few days early.
- Keep them in one folder on your phone and on a computer.
- Create your account and try the upload step early, even if you submit later.
- Save or screenshot the confirmation page and fee receipt.

## Keep your documents private

Your CNIC, signature and certificates are sensitive. Lumi Pix processes files on your own device and never uploads them, so you can prepare everything safely, even on a shared computer, as long as you delete the downloaded files afterwards.

## Reusing your files

Once you've got a clean set, keep the **originals**, not just the compressed copies. Next time the limits may be different, and compressing the original to the new limit gives a better result than squeezing an already compressed file. Our guides on [compressing to 20KB](post:compress-image-to-20kb-guide), [compressing to 100KB](post:compress-image-to-100kb-guide) and [compressing a photo for online application forms](post:compress-photo-for-online-forms) cover each step in more detail.

This guide is general, practical advice for preparing files. It doesn't replace the official instructions, which can change between advertisements. Always go by the organisation's own website and advertisement for the current requirements.

## The short version

- **Read the official instructions** for each advertisement and note every limit.
- **Get your photo, signature and documents ready early**, before the portal gets busy.
- **Keep the originals** and compress them again for each new application.

## Frequently asked questions

### What photo size is required for PPSC, FPSC or NTS?

It depends on the advertisement, and the requirements can change. Check the official instructions for your post, then compress to that limit.

### Can I prepare my photo on a mobile phone?

Yes. All the Lumi Pix tools work in mobile browsers, so you can crop, resize and compress on your phone before you upload.

### Is Lumi Pix an official PPSC, FPSC or NTS tool?

No. Lumi Pix is an independent image tool and isn't connected to any of these organisations.

### Can I use the same photo for different job portals?

Often yes, if it's recent and meets each portal's rules. Keep the original and compress it again to each new limit.

### What should I do if the portal is not accepting my upload near the deadline?

Busy portals can be slow. Get your files ready early, try again later, and contact the organisation's help desk if it keeps happening.
