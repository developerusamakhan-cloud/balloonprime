Recruitment and testing portals in Pakistan, such as PPSC, FPSC and NTS, ask candidates to upload a photograph, a signature and often scanned documents. Each upload has rules on size and format, and a rejected file can cost you time near a deadline. This checklist helps you prepare everything before you start the form.

**Important:** requirements are set by each organisation and can change between advertisements. Always read the instructions for the specific post you are applying for. Lumi Pix is an independent tool and is not affiliated with PPSC, FPSC, NTS or any government department.

## Before you start: read the instructions

From the advertisement or the portal's help page, note down:

- photo **file size limit** (in KB) and **format** (usually JPG)
- photo **dimensions** or proportions, if stated
- **background colour** for the photo
- signature size limit and format
- which **documents** need to be uploaded, and their limits

## The photograph

1. Take a recent photo against a plain, light background, facing the camera, in even light.
2. Crop to head and shoulders, or use the [passport photo maker](tool:passport-size-photo) if specific proportions are required.
3. Compress to the limit from the instructions. There are dedicated pages for [PPSC](tool:compress-photo-for-ppsc), [FPSC](tool:compress-photo-for-fpsc) and [NTS](tool:compress-photo-for-nts) with quick size buttons, or use the general [Image Compressor](tool:compress-image).
4. Open the result and check your face is sharp.

## The signature

1. Sign with a dark pen on plain white paper.
2. Photograph it from directly above in good light and crop tightly.
3. Use the [signature resizer](tool:resize-signature) and choose the limit from the instructions.

Full details are in our [signature resizing guide](post:resize-signature-for-online-form).

## Document scans

CNIC copies, degrees, transcripts and domicile certificates should be flat, well lit and fully readable. Compress each one with a limit such as [200KB](tool:compress-image-to-200kb), following our guide to [compressing document scans](post:compress-document-scan-to-200kb). Check that numbers and dates remain legible.

## Final checklist before uploading

- Photo: correct size limit, JPG, plain background, face clear
- Signature: correct size limit, JPG, dark and readable
- Documents: readable, under the limit, correct format
- File names are simple, without special characters
- Copies of all files saved in one folder for future applications

## If an upload fails

- **"File too large":** choose a slightly smaller limit; some portals count 1KB as 1,000 bytes.
- **"Invalid format":** make sure the file ends in .jpg. iPhone photos may be HEIC; Lumi Pix saves JPG automatically.
- **"Invalid dimensions":** resize to the stated size with the [Image Resizer](tool:image-resizer), then compress again.
- **Portal errors near deadlines:** busy portals can be slow. Prepare your files early and try again later if the site is overloaded.

## Keep your documents private

Your CNIC, signature and certificates are sensitive. Lumi Pix processes files on your own device and never uploads them, so you can prepare everything safely, even on a shared computer, as long as you delete the downloaded files afterwards.
