A transparent signature is handy for signing PDFs, letters and invoices, and some online forms ask for a clean signature scan. Here is how to get a crisp, transparent signature from a simple phone photo.

## Step 1: Sign on clean paper

- Use **plain white paper** with no lines.
- Sign with a **dark blue or black pen**. A felt-tip or gel pen gives a bolder line than a ballpoint.
- Sign a little larger than usual. It is easier to shrink a big signature cleanly than to enlarge a small one.

## Step 2: Take a good photo

- Use **daylight** or a bright, even lamp. Avoid casting your phone's shadow over the paper.
- Hold the phone **directly above** the paper, parallel to it, so the signature is not skewed.
- Fill most of the frame with the signature, then crop closely around it.

## Step 3: Remove the white background

1. Open the [white background remover](tool:remove-white-background).
2. Drop the photo. White areas turn transparent immediately.
3. If light grey patches remain, raise **Tolerance** a little.
4. If the edges look rough, raise **Edge softness**.
5. Download as **PNG**. PNG keeps the transparency; JPG would fill it with white again.

Paper in photos is rarely pure white. If the background has a cream or grey tint, click **Pick from image** and then click on the paper to remove that exact colour.

The tool also removes the faint white halo around the ink that simpler tools leave behind, so the signature looks natural on coloured backgrounds.

## Step 4: Resize if needed

For documents, a signature around 600 pixels wide is plenty. For online forms with strict limits, use the [signature resizer](tool:resize-signature), which compresses the file to a size such as 20KB.

Note that many forms ask for a **JPG** signature on a white background rather than a transparent PNG. In that case you do not need to remove the background at all: just crop, then resize. Our guide to [compressing photos for online forms](post:compress-photo-for-online-forms) covers the details.

## Using your transparent signature

- **Word or Google Docs:** insert the PNG and set text wrapping to "In front of text" to place it over a signature line.
- **PDF editors:** most allow adding an image as a signature or stamp.
- **Email signatures:** keep it small, around 200 pixels wide.

Keep the file somewhere private. A signature image is personal, which is one reason a tool that processes the file on your own device is a sensible choice.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I make my signature transparent?

Photograph it on white paper, drop it into a white background remover and download the result as a PNG.

### Why does my signature still have a white box around it?

The file was probably saved as JPG, which cannot store transparency. Download as PNG.

### What is the best format for a signature?

PNG for a transparent signature on documents. JPG with a white background when an online form asks for it.
