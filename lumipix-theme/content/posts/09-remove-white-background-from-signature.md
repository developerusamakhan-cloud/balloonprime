A transparent signature is really handy for signing PDFs, letters and invoices, and some online forms ask for a clean signature scan too. Here's how to remove the white background from a signature and turn a simple phone photo into a crisp, transparent PNG.

## Step 1: Sign on clean paper

- Use **plain white paper** with no lines.
- Sign with a **black or dark blue pen**. A felt-tip or gel pen gives a bolder line than a ballpoint.
- Sign a bit bigger than usual. It's easier to shrink a big signature cleanly than to enlarge a small one.

## Step 2: Take a good photo

- Use **daylight** or a bright, even lamp, and watch that your phone doesn't cast a shadow on the paper.
- Hold the phone **straight above** the paper, flat and parallel to it, so the signature isn't skewed.
- Fill most of the frame with the signature, then crop close around it.

## Step 3: Remove the white background

1. Open the tool to [remove white background from image](tool:remove-white-background) files.
2. Drop the photo in. The white areas turn transparent straight away.
3. If light grey patches are left, raise **Tolerance** a little.
4. If the edges look rough, raise **Edge softness**.
5. Download as **PNG**. PNG keeps the transparency. JPG would fill it with white again.

Paper in photos is hardly ever pure white. If yours has a cream or grey tint, click **Pick from image** and then click the paper to remove that exact colour.

The tool also gets rid of the faint white halo around the ink that simpler tools leave behind, so your signature looks natural on coloured backgrounds too.

## Step 4: Resize it if you need to

For documents, a signature about 600 pixels wide is plenty. For online forms with strict limits, [resize signature online](tool:resize-signature) to a size like 20KB.

Worth knowing: many forms want a **JPG** signature on a white background, not a transparent PNG. In that case you don't need to remove the background at all. Just crop and resize. Our guide on [how to resize a signature for an online form](post:resize-signature-for-online-form) covers the details.

## Using your transparent signature

- **Word or Google Docs.** Insert the PNG and set text wrapping to "In front of text" so you can place it over the signature line.
- **PDF editors.** Most let you add an image as a signature or stamp.
- **Email signatures.** Keep it small, around 200 pixels wide.

Keep the file somewhere private. A signature image is personal, which is a good reason to use a tool that processes the file on your own device.

## Paper or screen?

There are two ways to get a signature image:

| Method | Good | Not so good |
|---|---|---|
| Sign on paper and photograph it | Looks exactly like your real signature | Needs good light and a clean background |
| Draw on a phone or tablet screen | Already on a clean background | Finger signatures can look shaky |

For official documents, a photographed paper signature usually looks more natural. A stylus on a tablet is a nice middle ground.

## Fixing common problems

**Grey or uneven paper.** Raise **Tolerance** until the paper disappears. If the ink starts fading, lower it again and brighten the photo in your phone's editor before removing the background.

**Shadows across the page.** Retake the photo with light coming from the side, or near a window, and hold the phone higher.

**Thin or broken lines.** The ink is too light. Sign again with a gel or felt-tip pen.

**Blue lines from ruled paper.** Use plain paper if you can. If you've only got ruled paper, pick the line colour with **Pick from image** first, download, then run the result through again picking white.

**Jagged edges.** Raise **Edge softness** a little. The tool blends the edge pixels so the signature looks smooth on any background.

## Keeping your signature safe

A signature image can be misused if it ends up in the wrong hands. A few habits help:

- Don't upload your signature to websites you don't trust.
- Keep the PNG in a private folder, not on a shared desktop.
- Use a lower resolution copy for email signatures.
- Delete copies from shared or public computers when you're done.

Because Lumi Pix processes the image in your browser, your signature is never sent to our servers.

For documents, letters and PDFs, a **transparent PNG** sits naturally on the page. Keep both in the same folder, along with the original photo, and you'll never have to start from scratch.

## The short version

- **Sign on plain white paper** with a bold, dark pen, and photograph it from straight above in good light.
- **Crop tight** before removing the background. Less paper means a cleaner result.
- **Use tolerance** to get rid of grey paper, and **edge softness** to smooth jagged lines.
- **Download as PNG** to keep the transparency. JPG always fills it with a colour.
- **Keep two versions:** a transparent PNG for documents and a small JPG on white for forms.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I make my signature transparent?

Photograph it on white paper, drop it into a white background remover and download the result as a PNG.

### Why does my signature still have a white box around it?

It was probably saved as JPG, which can't store transparency. Download it as PNG.

### What is the best format for a signature?

PNG for a transparent signature on documents. JPG with a white background when an online form asks for it.

### Can I use a transparent signature in Google Docs?

Yes. Insert the PNG and set it to appear in front of text so you can place it over the signature line.

### What pen is best for a signature scan?

A black or dark blue gel or felt-tip pen gives bold, even lines that stay clear after removing the background and compressing.
