A logo with a white box around it looks unprofessional on coloured websites, slides and shirts. The fix is a transparent background. If your logo sits on a flat colour, you can make it transparent in seconds.

## The quick method

1. Open the [white background remover](tool:remove-white-background).
2. Drop your logo. White areas become transparent immediately.
3. If the background is not white, click **Pick from image** and click on the background.
4. Adjust **Tolerance** until the background disappears completely without eating into the logo.
5. Adjust **Edge softness** for smooth edges.
6. Download as **PNG**.

The preview shows the result on a checkerboard pattern, which represents transparency.

## Getting clean edges

Logos are usually anti-aliased: the pixels along the edge are a blend of the logo colour and the background. Simple tools either leave a light fringe or cut the edges harshly. The Lumi Pix remover fades edge pixels smoothly and **removes the background colour from them**, so the logo looks right on dark backgrounds too.

If you see a faint halo:

- increase **Edge softness** slightly
- make sure you picked the actual background colour, not a nearby shade

## When the logo contains the background colour

If your logo has white parts inside it, such as white text on a red circle, those parts would also turn transparent. In that case:

- choose a lower tolerance so only pure background is removed, and check the inside areas, or
- use the AI [Background Remover](tool:background-remover), which detects the object rather than a colour, or
- go back to the original design file and export with transparency, which is always the cleanest option.

## Which file format?

- **PNG** keeps transparency and works everywhere.
- **WebP** also supports transparency with smaller files, ideal for websites.
- **JPG does not support transparency.** If you save as JPG, the background fills with a colour again.

Our [PNG vs JPG guide](post:png-vs-jpg) explains the formats in more depth.

## Logo from a photo or a busy background

If the logo is photographed on a sign, a shirt or a textured surface, colour-based removal will struggle. Use the AI [Background Remover](tool:background-remover), or ask the designer for the original file. For more methods, see [how to remove the background from an image](post:how-to-remove-background-from-image).

## Checking that the background is really transparent

A checkerboard preview means transparency, but some image viewers show transparent areas as white or black, which can be confusing. To be sure:

- Open the PNG in a browser tab; transparent areas show the page colour.
- Place it on a coloured slide in PowerPoint or Google Slides.
- Look at it in dark mode; any leftover white box will stand out immediately.

## Preparing logos for different uses

| Use | Format | Size |
|---|---|---|
| Website header | PNG or SVG | Around 2× the displayed height, e.g. 120 px tall for a 60 px header |
| Favicon | PNG | 512 × 512, square |
| Social media profile | PNG or JPG | 400 × 400 or larger, square |
| Email signature | PNG | 200–300 px wide |
| Printing | PNG at 300 DPI, or vector | Depends on the print size |
| Watermark on photos | PNG with transparency | Around 15–25% of the photo width |

You can produce each of these from one transparent PNG with the [Image Resizer](tool:image-resizer). Choose **PNG** under **Save as** to keep the transparency.

## Logos on dark backgrounds

A dark logo on a dark website disappears even with a transparent background. Common fixes:

- Create a **light version** of the logo for dark backgrounds.
- Add a subtle **white outline** or shadow in a design tool.
- Place the logo on a light **badge or circle** that sits on the dark background.

If your site has a dark mode, prepare both versions now so you are not caught out later.

## Vector logos are even better

If your logo was designed in Illustrator, Figma or a similar tool, ask for an **SVG** or **PDF** version. Vector files are sharp at any size and already transparent. Use a PNG when an app does not accept vectors, and export it at a generous size so it stays crisp.

## Cleaning up a scanned logo

Logos that only exist on paper, such as old letterheads or stamps, can be digitised:

1. Scan or photograph the logo flat in good light.
2. Crop tightly, then brighten and increase contrast so the paper is close to white.
3. Use the [white background remover](tool:remove-white-background) to make the paper transparent.
4. If the edges look rough, increase **Edge softness**.

For a perfectly clean result, a designer can trace the scan into a vector logo.

## Logo with a coloured background

If the logo sits on a solid coloured box, for example white text on a blue rectangle, decide which part you want to keep. To remove the blue, click **Pick from image** and then the blue area. To keep the box and remove only the surrounding white, pick white instead. The same technique works for stamps and badges. For more background removal methods, including photos, see [how to remove the background from an image](post:how-to-remove-background-from-image) and our guide to [transparent signatures](post:remove-white-background-from-signature).

## Key takeaways

- **Flat backgrounds** can be removed instantly with a colour-key tool; busy ones need AI.
- **Pick the exact background colour** if it is not pure white.
- **Use edge softness** to avoid jagged edges and light halos.
- **Save as PNG or WebP** to keep transparency; JPG will fill it again.
- **Prepare a light version** of your logo for dark backgrounds and dark mode.
- **Ask for a vector file** (SVG or PDF) whenever possible for the sharpest results at any size.
- **Test the result** on white, black and coloured backgrounds before using it everywhere.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I remove the white background from a logo?

Drop the logo into a white background remover, adjust tolerance if needed, and download it as a PNG.

### Why does my transparent logo have a white outline?

The edge pixels still contain some white. Increase edge softness or use a tool that removes the background colour from the edges.

### Can I make a JPG logo transparent?

Yes. Remove the background and save the result as PNG, because JPG cannot store transparency.

### What size should a transparent logo be?

Export it at about twice the size it is displayed, for example 120 pixels tall for a 60-pixel header, so it stays sharp on high-resolution screens.

### Is SVG better than PNG for logos?

For websites and printing, SVG is sharper at any size. Use PNG when an app or platform does not accept SVG.

### Can I make a logo transparent on my phone?

Yes. Open the white background remover in your mobile browser, choose the logo from your gallery and download the PNG. It works the same way as on a computer.
