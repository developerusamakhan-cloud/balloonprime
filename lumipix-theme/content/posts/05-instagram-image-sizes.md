Instagram resizes and recompresses everything you upload. If your image does not match the sizes it expects, it crops the edges or softens the detail. Uploading at the right size gives you control over what people actually see.

## Instagram image sizes at a glance

| Format | Aspect ratio | Recommended size |
|---|---|---|
| Portrait post | 4:5 | 1080 × 1350 px |
| Square post | 1:1 | 1080 × 1080 px |
| Landscape post | 1.91:1 | 1080 × 566 px |
| Story and reel | 9:16 | 1080 × 1920 px |
| Profile picture | 1:1 | 320 × 320 px or larger |

Instagram displays feed images at a width of 1080 pixels, so there is no benefit in uploading wider files. They are scaled down anyway.

## Which post shape should you use?

**Portrait (4:5)** takes up the most space in the feed, which makes it the most eye-catching choice for photos of people, products and food.

**Square (1:1)** is the classic Instagram format and works well for graphics, quotes and carousels where consistency matters.

**Landscape (1.91:1)** suits wide scenery but appears small in the feed.

Instagram has also moved the profile grid to taller rectangular thumbnails. Portrait images crop more gracefully in the grid than square or landscape ones, which is another reason 4:5 is a strong default. Platform specifications change from time to time, so check the app before a big campaign.

## Stories and reels

Stories and reels fill the phone screen at 9:16, so 1080×1920 is the size to use. Keep important text and faces away from the top and bottom 250 pixels or so, where the profile name, buttons and captions sit.

## How to post a full photo without cropping

Instagram crops anything outside the allowed ratios. To keep the whole picture:

1. Open the [Instagram resizer](tool:resize-image-for-instagram).
2. Tap **Instagram portrait 1080×1350** (or square).
3. Set **Fit** to *Fit – add background* and pick a colour: white, black, or a colour from your brand.
4. Download and post. The entire photo is visible, with neat borders where needed.

If you would rather fill the frame, choose **Fill – crop edges** instead. The resizer crops from the centre.

## Keep your photos sharp

- **Upload at 1080 pixels wide.** Larger files are recompressed more aggressively.
- **Avoid uploading screenshots of photos.** They have already lost detail.
- **Export as high-quality JPG.** Instagram converts everything anyway, so a clean JPG gives it the best starting point.
- **Check the app's upload quality setting.** Instagram has an option to upload at the highest quality, which helps on slower connections.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best image size for Instagram?

1080 × 1350 pixels (4:5 portrait) gives the largest presence in the feed. Use 1080 × 1080 for square posts and 1080 × 1920 for stories and reels.

### Why does Instagram make my photos blurry?

Usually because the upload was much larger or smaller than 1080 pixels wide, or was already heavily compressed. Upload a clean 1080-pixel-wide JPG.

### How do I fit a whole picture on Instagram?

Resize it to 1080 × 1350 or 1080 × 1080 using a fit mode that adds a background around the photo, so nothing is cropped.
