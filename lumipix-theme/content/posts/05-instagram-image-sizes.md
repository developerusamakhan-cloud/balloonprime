Instagram resizes and recompresses everything you post. If your image doesn't match the sizes it expects, it crops the edges or softens the detail. Uploading at the right Instagram image size puts you back in charge of what people actually see.

## Instagram image sizes at a glance

| Format | Aspect ratio | Recommended size |
|---|---|---|
| Portrait post | 4:5 | 1080 x 1350 px |
| Square post | 1:1 | 1080 x 1080 px |
| Landscape post | 1.91:1 | 1080 x 566 px |
| Story and reel | 9:16 | 1080 x 1920 px |
| Profile picture | 1:1 | 320 x 320 px or bigger |

Instagram shows feed images 1080 pixels wide, so there's no point uploading anything wider. It gets scaled down anyway.

## Which shape should you post?

**Portrait (4:5)** takes up the most room in the feed, so it's the most eye-catching choice for people, products and food.

**Square (1:1)** is the classic Instagram shape. It works well for graphics, quotes and carousels where you want everything to match.

**Landscape (1.91:1)** suits wide scenery, but it looks small in the feed.

Instagram has also switched the profile grid to taller rectangular thumbnails. Portrait images crop much more nicely there than square or landscape ones, which is another reason 4:5 is a good default. Instagram changes its specs from time to time, so check the app before a big campaign.

## Stories and reels

Stories and reels fill the phone screen at 9:16, so 1080 x 1920 is the size to use. Keep important text and faces away from the very top and bottom, where your name, the buttons and captions sit.

## Keeping your photos sharp

- **Upload at 1080 pixels wide.** Bigger files get recompressed harder.
- **Don't post screenshots of photos.** They've already lost detail.
- **Export a high quality JPG.** Instagram converts everything anyway, so a clean JPG gives it the best start.
- **Check the upload quality setting in the app.** There's an option to upload at the highest quality, which helps on slower connections.

## Carousels: keep every slide the same shape

A carousel uses the shape of the **first** image for every slide. If the first is 4:5 portrait and a later one is square, the square one gets cropped or padded. To stay in control:

1. Pick one shape for the whole carousel, usually 1080 x 1350.
2. Drop all the images into the Instagram resizer together.
3. Choose the portrait preset and **Fit** so nothing gets cut off.
4. Click **Download all** and upload them in order.

## Profile picture tips

Your profile picture is a small circle, often under 110 pixels on screen. Upload a square image of at least 320 x 320 pixels, keep your face in the middle, and leave some space around it so the circle doesn't chop your hair or chin. A plain or softly blurred background keeps it readable at that size. Want a clean background? [Remove background for free online](tool:background-remover) and pick a solid colour.

## Safe zones for stories and reels

Stories and reels put your name and buttons over the picture. As a rough rule:

| Area | What covers it | Keep clear of |
|---|---|---|
| Top 250 px or so | Your name, the progress bar | Text and faces |
| Bottom 340 px or so | Reply bar, captions, buttons | Calls to action, small text |
| Right edge | Like, comment and share icons on reels | Important details |

Design in the middle of the frame and your message will show on any phone.

## Text on images

Instagram compresses images, and that can make small text fuzzy. For quote posts and infographics:

- use big, bold fonts
- leave at least 60 pixels of margin from the edges
- export as PNG if the design has flat colours, because PNG keeps edges sharper before Instagram converts it

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best image size for Instagram?

1080 x 1350 pixels (4:5 portrait) gives you the biggest presence in the feed. Use 1080 x 1080 for square posts and 1080 x 1920 for stories and reels.

### Why does Instagram make my photos blurry?

Usually because the upload was much bigger or smaller than 1080 pixels wide, or it was already heavily compressed. Upload a clean JPG that's 1080 pixels wide.

### How do I fit a whole picture on Instagram?

Resize it to 1080 x 1350 or 1080 x 1080 with a fit mode that adds a background around the photo, so nothing gets cropped.

### What size is an Instagram carousel?

Use the same size for every slide, usually 1080 x 1350 for portrait or 1080 x 1080 for square. The first image sets the shape for all of them.

### How do I stop Instagram cropping my profile picture?

Upload a square image with your face in the middle and some space around it, at least 320 x 320 pixels.
