**Last updated: October 2026**

This Cookie Policy explains how [lumipix_site] uses cookies and similar technologies, and how you can control them. It should be read together with our [Privacy Policy](page:privacy).

## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies include local storage and the browser cache. They help websites remember preferences, measure traffic and show relevant advertising.

## Cookies and storage we use

| Type | Purpose | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Strictly necessary | Keep the site working and remember choices you make | Your light or dark theme choice (local storage) |
| Consent | Remember your cookie choices for 180 days | The lumipix_consent cookie |
| Preferences | Show our occasional studio message no more than once a day | lumipix_lead_seen and lumipix_lead_time (local storage) |
| Browser cache | Store the background remover's AI model so it does not download again | Cache storage used by the Transformers.js library |
| Analytics (if enabled) | Measure visits and popular tools in aggregate | Google Analytics cookies such as _ga |
| Advertising (if enabled) | Show and measure ads that keep the tools free | Google AdSense cookies |

Lumi Pix does not use cookies to read or store your images. Images are processed in your browser and are never uploaded.

## Third-party cookies

Advertising and analytics providers, such as Google, may set their own cookies when ads or analytics are enabled. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google's Ads Settings (adssettings.google.com) and learn about other choices at www.aboutads.info/choices.

## Your choices

- **Consent:** where the law requires it, we ask for your consent before non-essential cookies are set, and you can change your choice at any time with the **Cookie settings** link at the bottom of every page.
- **Browser settings:** you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking all cookies may affect how some features work.
- **Clearing stored data:** clearing your browser's site data removes the stored theme preference and the cached AI model. The model will download again the next time you use the background remover.

## Changes

We may update this Cookie Policy when we change the services we use. The date at the top shows the latest version.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I use Lumi Pix with cookies blocked?

Yes. The image tools work without cookies. Blocking storage may only reset your theme preference and require the AI model to download again.

### Are cookies used to look at my images?

No. Images are processed in your browser and are never read by cookies or sent to our servers.
