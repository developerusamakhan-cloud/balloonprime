**Last updated: October 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains what information [lumipix_site] ("Lumi Pix", "we", "us") collects when you use this website and its image tools, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the site you agree to the practices described here.

## 1. The short version

- **Your images are never uploaded.** All image processing (compressing, resizing, background removal) happens inside your web browser on your own device. We do not receive, see or store your images.
- We collect limited technical information that every website receives, such as your IP address and browser type, to keep the site running and secure.
- If analytics or advertising are enabled, third parties such as Google may use cookies. You can control these as described below.
- We do not sell your personal information.

## 2. Images and files you use with our tools

When you choose, drop or paste an image into a Lumi Pix tool, the file is read by your browser and processed locally using your device's own resources. The file and the result are not transmitted to our servers or to any third party. When you close or reload the page, the images are cleared from the page's memory. Downloads are saved directly from your browser to your device.

The background remover downloads an AI model and a code library the first time you use it (see section 5). This download contains no information about your images.

## 3. Information we collect

**Information collected automatically.** Like most websites, our hosting provider records standard server log data when you visit a page, such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring page, the pages you request and the date and time of the request. We use this information to operate the site, prevent abuse, fix errors and understand overall traffic.

**Analytics.** We may use an analytics service, such as Google Analytics, to understand how visitors use the site in aggregate (for example, which tools are most popular). Analytics services use cookies or similar technologies and receive information such as your IP address, device information and pages visited.

**Information you send us.** If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and any details you choose to include. We use this only to respond to you and to improve the service.

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect names, payment details or identity documents.

## 4. Cookies and advertising

We may display advertising provided by Google AdSense or similar networks to keep the tools free.

- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
- Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
- You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings (adssettings.google.com). You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info/choices.

Where required by law, for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, we ask for your consent before non-essential cookies are used. More details are in our [Cookie Policy](page:cookies).

## 5. Third-party services

- **Content delivery network.** The AI background remover loads the Transformers.js library from jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net) and downloads the AI model from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). These providers receive standard technical data, such as your IP address, when your browser requests these files. They do not receive your images.
- **Advertising and analytics providers** such as Google, as described above.
- **Hosting provider**, which stores server logs as part of running the website.

These providers process data under their own privacy policies.

## 6. How we use information

We use the limited information we collect to:

- provide, maintain and secure the website and tools
- understand which features are used so we can improve them
- show advertising that helps keep the service free
- respond to messages you send us
- comply with legal obligations

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, our legal bases are our legitimate interests in running and improving a secure website, your consent for non-essential cookies, and compliance with legal obligations.

## 7. Data retention

We keep server logs and emails only as long as needed for the purposes above, after which they are deleted or anonymised. Analytics and advertising providers retain data according to their own policies and settings.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Residents of California have the right to know what personal information is collected and to request its deletion, and we do not sell personal information. To exercise any of these rights, email us at [lumipix_email].

## 9. Children's privacy

Lumi Pix is a general-audience website and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. Security

Because your images are processed locally, they are not exposed to risks on our servers. We use HTTPS to protect the connection between your browser and our website. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect the information we do hold.

## 11. International visitors

Our website and service providers may process technical data in countries other than your own. Where required, appropriate safeguards are used for such transfers.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed. Significant changes will be highlighted on this page.

## 13. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at [lumipix_email].

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Lumi Pix upload or store my images?

No. Images are processed inside your browser on your own device. They are never sent to or stored on our servers.

### Does Lumi Pix use cookies?

The site stores your light or dark theme choice in your browser. If analytics or advertising are enabled, providers such as Google may set cookies, as explained in the Cookie Policy.

### How can I ask for my data to be deleted?

Email us at the address on this page. Because we do not collect images or accounts, we usually hold very little information about you.
