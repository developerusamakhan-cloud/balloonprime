## Image tools without the catch

Lumi Pix started with a simple frustration. Most online image tools upload your photos to a server, cap what you can do for free, or hide the full-resolution download behind a signup. We think everyday image tasks should be instant, free and private.

So we built tools that run where your photos already are: on your own phone or computer. When you compress, resize or remove a background with Lumi Pix, the work happens inside your browser. Your images are never sent to us.

## What we believe

- **Your files are yours.** We do not need to see your photos to help you resize them.
- **Free should mean free.** No "HD costs extra", no watermark and no account wall.
- **Do one job well.** Each tool opens ready for a specific task, with sensible defaults.
- **Plain language.** Guides explain what DPI, KB and aspect ratios mean without jargon.

## How it works

Modern browsers can decode, edit and encode images on their own. Lumi Pix uses that built-in capability for compression and resizing. The background remover uses a small AI model that your browser downloads once and then runs locally.

## How we keep it free

Because there are no servers processing images, Lumi Pix costs very little to run. The site may show a small number of unobtrusive ads to cover hosting and development. Ads never sit on top of a tool or block a download.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who runs Lumi Pix?

Lumi Pix is an independent project focused on simple, private image tools. It is not affiliated with any government body, exam board or software company.

### Do you store my images?

No. Images are processed on your device and are never uploaded to our servers.

### How does Lumi Pix make money?

Through a small number of ads on the website. In the future we may offer optional extras, but the core tools will stay free.
