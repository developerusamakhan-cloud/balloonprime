Lumi Pix is a set of free image tools that compress, resize and remove backgrounds right in your browser, with nothing uploaded.

## What Lumi Pix does

Lumi Pix is a set of free image tools for the jobs that come up every week: a form that rejects your photo for being "too large", an Instagram post that crops off someone's head, a logo that needs a transparent background, or a print that comes out blurry. Each tool does one job well and opens ready to use. There is nothing to install, no account to create and no watermark on the result.

The difference is where the work happens. Most online editors upload your photo to a server, process it there and send it back. Lumi Pix does the processing **inside your browser**, on your own phone or computer. Your images never travel across the internet, which makes the tools faster on slow connections and keeps private documents private.

## Compress an image to an exact file size

Upload limits are everywhere: job portals, university admissions, visa applications, government recruitment forms, email attachments and marketplaces. The usual advice is to "lower the quality" and try again, which wastes time and often leaves you with a blurry file.

The [Image Compressor](tool:compress-image) works the other way round. You type the size you need, for example 20KB, 50KB, 100KB or 200KB, and the tool searches for the sharpest version of your photo that fits under that limit. If lowering the quality is not enough, it reduces the dimensions in small steps so faces and text stay readable. You can drop up to 20 photos at once, and each one is compressed to the same target.

Popular sizes have their own ready-made pages: [compress to 20KB](tool:compress-image-to-20kb) for signatures and small profile photos, [compress to 50KB](tool:compress-image-to-50kb) for passport-style photographs, [compress to 100KB](tool:compress-image-to-100kb) for most application forms, [compress to 200KB](tool:compress-image-to-200kb) for document scans and [compress to 1MB](tool:compress-image-to-1mb) when you want to keep almost all of the detail.

## Resize images in pixels, centimetres or inches

Screens measure images in pixels, while printers and many official forms measure them in centimetres, millimetres or inches. The [Image Resizer](tool:image-resizer) handles both. Choose a unit, type the size and set the DPI (dots per inch) when you are printing. Lumi Pix calculates the pixels for you and writes the DPI into the JPG file, so it prints at the size you asked for.

Three fit modes cover every situation:

- **Fit** keeps the whole picture and fills the empty space with a colour you choose.
- **Fill** crops the edges so the image fills the frame exactly.
- **Stretch** forces the exact size, for the rare case where distortion does not matter.

One-tap presets cover the sizes people ask for most. The [Instagram resizer](tool:resize-image-for-instagram) has the portrait 1080×1350, square 1080×1080 and story 1080×1920 sizes. The [passport photo maker](tool:passport-size-photo) produces 35×45 mm and 2×2 inch photos at 300 DPI. The [signature resizer](tool:resize-signature) makes a clean, small file for online applications.

## Remove backgrounds with on-device AI

The [Background Remover](tool:background-remover) uses an AI model that runs in your browser. The first time you use it, the model downloads once and is then cached. After that, photos are processed on your device and you can download a **full-resolution transparent PNG** for free. You can also replace the background with white, black or any colour, which is useful for profile photos and product images.

For logos, signatures, stamps and scans on white paper, you do not need AI at all. The [white background remover](tool:remove-white-background) turns white, or any colour you click on, transparent in an instant. It keeps soft edges and removes the light halo that simple tools leave behind.

## Why it matters that your photos stay on your device

The images people resize and compress are often personal: ID card scans, passport photos, certificates, signatures and family pictures. When a tool uploads them, you have to trust that the service deletes them, does not reuse them and keeps them secure. With Lumi Pix there is nothing to trust, because the files are never sent to us.

Running on your device has two more benefits. It is **fast**, because there is no waiting for a 5MB photo to upload and download again. It also keeps the service **free**: without servers processing every image, Lumi Pix costs very little to run, so the full-quality result does not need to sit behind a paywall.

## Tips for better results

- **Start from the original file.** A screenshot of a photo has already lost detail.
- **Crop first, then compress.** Fewer pixels means more quality per kilobyte.
- **Check the exact requirement.** Forms state limits in KB, pixels or centimetres. Read the official instructions, then choose the matching tool.
- **Use JPG for photos and PNG for graphics.** JPG keeps photos small; PNG keeps logos and transparent backgrounds sharp.
- **Use 300 DPI for prints.** For screens, only the pixel size matters.
