Contact Lumi Pix to report a bug, suggest a new tool or ask a question about an image task.

## We would love to hear from you

Found a bug, have an idea for a new tool, or want to ask a question about how something works? Send us an email and we will get back to you, usually within two working days.

**Email:** [lumipix_email]

## Before you write

- **A tool is not working?** Tell us which tool, your browser and device, and what happened. A screenshot helps.
- **Privacy question?** Our [Privacy Policy](page:privacy) explains what we collect and what we never see.
- **Partnerships and advertising:** include your company name and a short description of the proposal.

Please do not send us photos or documents that contain personal information. We do not need them to help you, and the tools never upload your images.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you help me with a specific form's photo requirements?

We can explain how to use the tools, but only the organisation running the form can confirm its exact requirements. Always check the official instructions.

### Can I request a new tool?

Yes. Tell us what you are trying to do and which sizes or formats you need. We prioritise the most requested tools.
