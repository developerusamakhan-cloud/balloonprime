## Getting your photo ready for PPSC

The Punjab Public Service Commission recruits for Government of Punjab posts through its online application system. Each application asks for a photograph, and usually a scanned signature and documents too. Every file has its own rules for size, format and sometimes shape. Get one wrong and the upload simply fails.

Pick the size limit from the instructions for your post, drop your photo, and download the result. That's it.

**Please note:** the requirements can change from one advertisement to the next. Always read the current instructions on the official PPSC website or in the advertisement for your post. Lumi Pix is an independent tool and isn't connected to PPSC.

## Step by step

1. **Read the instructions** for your post. Note the photo limit in KB, the format and any dimensions.
2. **Take a suitable photo.** Recent, facing the camera, plain light background, even light.
3. **Crop it** to head and shoulders, or to the exact shape if dimensions are given. The [passport size photo creator](tool:passport-size-photo) can crop to sizes like 35 x 45 mm.
4. **Choose the limit** with the quick buttons or type it in.
5. **Drop the photo** and wait for **Fits target**.
6. **Download it** and upload the new file in the PPSC portal.

## Photo tips that save you a rejection

- **Use the back camera** and ask someone to take the photo from about a metre away.
- **Plain, light background** with no shadow behind your head.
- **Neutral face**, looking straight at the camera.
- **No filters** and no beauty mode.
- **A recent photo** that looks like you do now.

Background not plain? The [remove background for free online](tool:background-remover) tool can swap it for white before you crop and compress.

## Doing it on your phone

You can do the whole thing on a phone. Open this page in Chrome or Safari, tap **Choose files**, pick the cropped photo and download the result. Android saves it in **Downloads**, and iPhone saves it in the **Files** app. When the PPSC form asks for the photo, pick it from there, not from your gallery. Otherwise you'll upload the original by mistake.

## Still getting rejected?

- **"File size exceeds limit".** Pick a slightly lower limit. Some portals count 1KB as 1,000 bytes.
- **"Invalid file format".** Make sure you're uploading the JPG you downloaded.
- **"Invalid dimensions".** Set the pixel size in the [image resizer](tool:image-resizer), then compress again.
- **The portal is slow near the deadline.** Get your files ready early and try again later.

## A simple two-day plan

| When | What to do |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | Read the advertisement and list every file and its limit |
| Day 1 | Take the photo and signature, scan your CNIC and certificates |
| Day 2 | Crop, compress and name every file, then check them at 100% zoom |

Spreading it over two days gives you time to fix problems without the closing date breathing down your neck.

## Folders that save time next time

Make one folder per application, like "PPSC Lecturer English", with two folders inside: **originals** and **upload**. Camera photos and scans go in originals, compressed files go in upload. Next time, copy the originals and compress them to the new limits. That gives a sharper result than compressing files that are already small.

## What the reviewers need to see

They need to recognise your face, read your name, CNIC number and dates, and match your signature. Files squashed so hard that the text can't be read can cause trouble later, so always check before you submit.

## Sizes on this page

The quick buttons cover the limits you'll see most on recruitment forms: 20KB, 30KB, 50KB, 100KB and 200KB. If your advertisement says something else, type it in. If it gives dimensions, resize first, then compress.
