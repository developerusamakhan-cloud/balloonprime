## Preparing your photo for a PPSC application

The Punjab Public Service Commission (PPSC) recruits candidates for posts in the Government of Punjab through its online application system. Each application asks for a photograph and usually a scanned signature and supporting documents, each with its own size and format rules. A file that is too large, in the wrong format or the wrong shape is rejected at the upload step.

This page helps you meet those rules in a minute. Choose the size limit from the instructions for your post, drop your photo, and download a JPG that fits. Everything happens in your browser, so your photo and documents are never uploaded to Lumi Pix.

**Important:** requirements can change between advertisements. Always read the current instructions on the official PPSC website or the advertisement for your post. Lumi Pix is an independent tool and is not affiliated with PPSC.

## Step by step

1. **Read the instructions** for your post and note the photo size limit (in KB), format and any dimensions.
2. **Take a suitable photo**: recent, front-facing, against a plain light background, with even lighting.
3. **Crop** to head and shoulders, or to the exact proportions if dimensions are given. The [passport size photo maker](tool:passport-size-photo) can crop to sizes such as 35×45 mm.
4. **Choose the size limit** on this page using the quick buttons or the custom field.
5. **Drop the photo** and wait for **Fits target**.
6. **Download** and upload the new file in the PPSC portal.

## Photo tips that avoid rejection

- **Use the rear camera** and ask someone to take the photo from about a metre away.
- **Plain, light background** with no shadows behind your head.
- **Neutral expression**, looking straight at the camera.
- **No filters or heavy editing.**
- **Recent photo** that looks like you today.

If your background is not plain, the [Background Remover](tool:background-remover) can replace it with white before you crop and compress.

## Preparing on a phone

You can do everything on a phone. Open this page in Chrome or Safari, tap **Choose files**, pick the cropped photo and download the result. On Android, it is saved in **Downloads**; on iPhone, in the **Files** app. In the PPSC form, choose the file from there rather than from your gallery, so you upload the compressed copy and not the original.

## If the upload is still rejected

- **"File size exceeds limit":** choose a slightly lower limit; portals may count 1KB as 1,000 bytes.
- **"Invalid file format":** make sure you upload the downloaded JPG.
- **"Invalid dimensions":** resize to the stated pixel size with the [Image Resizer](tool:image-resizer), then compress again.
- **Portal slow near the deadline:** prepare files early and try again later.

## Folder structure that saves time

Create one folder for each application, such as "PPSC – [post name]", with two subfolders: **originals** and **upload**. Keep camera photos and scans in originals, and compressed files in upload. Next time, copy the originals and compress them to the new limits.

## What reviewers look for

A clear photo and readable documents make processing easier. Reviewers need to recognise your face, read your name, CNIC number and dates, and match your signature. Over-compressed files with unreadable text can cause problems later in the process, so always check the result before submitting.

## Frequently prepared sizes

The quick buttons on this page cover the limits most often seen on recruitment forms: 20KB, 30KB, 50KB, 100KB and 200KB. If your advertisement mentions a different number, type it into the custom field. If it states dimensions, resize first, then compress.
