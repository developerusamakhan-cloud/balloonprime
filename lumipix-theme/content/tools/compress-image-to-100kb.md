## The most common upload limit

If you have filled in an online form recently, there is a good chance it asked for an image "under 100KB". Exam boards, job portals, visa services, university admissions and HR systems all use this limit. It is generous enough for a sharp portrait or a readable single-page document, yet small enough for servers to store millions of uploads.

This page opens with a 100KB target and a maximum width of 1600 pixels. Drop your image and the compressor finds the highest quality that fits, keeping as many pixels as it can.

## How to compress to 100KB

1. **Add your image.** Drag it onto the page, click **Choose files**, or paste it with Ctrl+V.
2. **Let it work.** In a second or two, the result shows its new size and a **Fits target** badge.
3. **Download.** Save one file or click **Download all** for a batch of up to 20.

Changing the target or format re-runs the compression automatically, so you can experiment freely.

## Typical results

| Original | Original size | Result at 100KB |
|---|---|---|
| Phone portrait 4032×3024 | 3–4MB | Around 1300–1600 px wide |
| DSLR photo 6000×4000 | 8–12MB | Around 1200–1500 px wide |
| A4 document scan | 1–3MB | Around 1500 px wide, readable text |
| Screenshot 1920×1080 | 500KB–1MB | Often full size |

Every image is different. Plain backgrounds and soft light compress better than detailed scenes.

## Best uses for 100KB

- **Application photos** where the form allows up to 100KB. You get a noticeably sharper result than at 50KB.
- **Single-page documents** such as a certificate, ID card or letter, where text must stay readable.
- **Profile pictures** for professional platforms and company directories.
- **Images on lightweight web pages**, such as blog thumbnails.

For multi-page or very detailed documents, a higher limit such as [200KB](tool:compress-image-to-200kb) is safer if the form allows it.

## Photos with dimensions as well as size

Some forms state both a file size and dimensions, for example "600×800 pixels, maximum 100KB". Resize first, then compress:

1. Use the [Image Resizer](tool:image-resizer) with the link icon off, type 600 and 800, and choose **Fill** to crop to shape.
2. Bring the downloaded file back here.
3. Open **Advanced options** and clear the maximum width, so the dimensions stay exactly as you set them.
4. Compress and download.

At 600×800, most portraits fit well under 100KB at high quality.

## Documents: keep text sharp

For documents, readable text matters more than pixel count. If small print looks fuzzy:

- Scan or photograph the page flat, in good light, and crop to the edges.
- Increase contrast slightly so text is darker and paper whiter.
- Set a **Maximum width** of about 1500–1600 pixels under **Advanced options**.

Our article on [how to lower image file size](post:how-to-lower-image-file-size) explains why fewer pixels at a good quality usually reads better than many pixels at a poor quality.

## Avoiding common upload errors

- **"File too large" at 99KB:** the portal may count 1KB as 1,000 bytes. Lumi Pix aims under that stricter number.
- **"Invalid file type":** upload the downloaded JPG, not an original HEIC or PNG.
- **Picked the wrong file on a phone:** choose the compressed copy from Downloads or the Files app, not from the gallery.
- **"Dimensions not allowed":** resize to the stated pixel size first.

## Where 100KB limits are common

- Job applications and recruitment portals
- University admissions and scholarship forms
- Visa and immigration applications in some countries
- Online exam registrations
- Professional profiles and staff directories
- Support tickets and help-desk attachments

## Photo or document: different priorities

For a **photo**, the face matters most. The compressor keeps as much resolution as it can at a good quality. For a **document**, every word must be readable. Set a maximum width of about 1500–1600 pixels under **Advanced options** so the quality stays high enough for small text. If a single page still looks fuzzy, the form may allow a higher limit for documents; check the instructions.

## Batch example

Imagine an application asking for six certificates, each under 100KB. Scan them, crop each page, drop all six here and click **Download all**. Rename them to match the form fields (for example `matric.jpg`, `intermediate.jpg`, `degree.jpg`) and upload. Because the processing happens on your device, even large scans are handled quickly without waiting for uploads.

## How 100KB looks on screen and in print

At around 1500 pixels wide, a 100KB portrait fills a laptop screen nicely and prints sharply at passport size. A document page at the same width is easily readable on screen. For prints larger than a postcard, keep the original file rather than the compressed copy.
