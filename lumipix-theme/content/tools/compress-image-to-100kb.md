Exam boards, job portals, visa services, universities and HR systems all love this number. It gives you enough room for a sharp portrait or a readable one-page document, while keeping files small enough for them to store millions.

This page opens with a 100KB target and a maximum width of 1600 pixels. The compressor keeps as many pixels as it can and finds the highest quality that still fits.

## How to compress an image to 100KB

1. **Add your image.** Drag it in, tap **Choose files**, or paste with Ctrl+V.
2. **Wait a second or two.** You'll see the new size and a **Fits target** badge.
3. **Download.** Save one file, or tap **Download all** for a batch of up to 20.

If you change the target or the format, it runs again straight away, so feel free to try things.

## When the form gives dimensions too

Some forms say both, like "600 x 800 pixels, maximum 100KB". Do the size first, then the compression:

1. Open the [image resizer](tool:image-resizer), turn the link icon off, type 600 and 800, and choose **Fill** to crop to shape.
2. Bring the downloaded file back here.
3. Open **Advanced options** and clear the maximum width so your dimensions stay exactly as set.
4. Compress and download.

At 600 x 800, most portraits come in well under 100KB at high quality.

## Keeping text sharp in documents

With documents, readable text matters more than the pixel count. If the small print looks fuzzy:

- Scan or photograph the page flat, in good light, and crop to the edges.
- Bump the contrast a little so the text is darker and the paper whiter.
- Set a **Maximum width** of about 1500 to 1600 pixels under **Advanced options**.

Our article on [how to lower image file size](post:how-to-lower-image-file-size) explains why fewer pixels at good quality reads better than lots of pixels at poor quality.

## Upload errors and what they mean

- **"File too large" at 99KB.** The portal may count 1KB as 1,000 bytes. Lumi Pix aims under that stricter number.
- **"Invalid file type".** Upload the JPG you downloaded, not an original HEIC or PNG.
- **Wrong file on a phone.** Choose the compressed copy from Downloads or the Files app, not the gallery.
- **"Dimensions not allowed".** Resize to the stated size in pixels first.
