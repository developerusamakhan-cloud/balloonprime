## Instant transparency for flat backgrounds

When an image has a plain white background, such as a scanned signature, a logo, a stamp or a drawing on paper, you do not need AI to remove it. This tool measures how close each pixel is to the background colour and fades it out smoothly, keeping the edges soft and natural. It works instantly, even on large scans, and it runs entirely in your browser.

## How to use it

1. **Drop your image.** White areas turn transparent immediately.
2. **Check the preview.** The checkerboard pattern on the right shows transparency.
3. **Adjust Tolerance** if light grey areas remain, for example paper that is not pure white.
4. **Adjust Edge softness** to smooth the transition around lines and shapes.
5. **Download as PNG** (or WebP) to keep the transparency.

## Removing colours other than white

The background does not have to be white. Click **Pick from image**, then click on the background in the original preview, and that colour becomes the one removed. You can also choose a colour with the colour picker. This works for logos on coloured boxes, green screens with even lighting, and stamps on tinted paper.

## Understanding the two sliders

| Slider | What it does | When to raise it | When to lower it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tolerance | How different a colour can be from the background and still be removed | Paper looks grey or uneven | Parts of the design start disappearing |
| Edge softness | How gradually pixels near the threshold fade | Edges look jagged | Edges look blurry or faint |

Start with the defaults, then adjust in small steps while watching the preview.

## No white halo

Simple tools often leave a light fringe around the edges because the edge pixels are a blend of the ink and the paper. This tool removes the background colour from those edge pixels too, so a dark signature or logo looks clean on any background, including dark mode and coloured slides.

## Best uses

- **Signatures** for Word documents, PDFs and letters. See [how to remove the white background from a signature](post:remove-white-background-from-signature).
- **Logos** that came as a JPG with a white box around them.
- **Stamps and seals** scanned from paper.
- **Line drawings and handwriting** for presentations.
- **Icons and simple graphics** on a solid background.

## When to use the AI remover instead

This tool removes a colour, not an object. If the background is a photo, a gradient, a pattern or a busy scene, use the AI [Background Remover](tool:background-remover), which detects the subject itself. Likewise, if your logo contains the same colour as the background (for example white text inside a shape), lower the tolerance carefully, or use the AI remover.

## Preparing scans for the best result

1. Scan or photograph the paper flat in even light.
2. Crop closely around the content.
3. Brighten the image slightly so the paper is near white.
4. Drop it here and fine-tune the sliders.

## Formats and sizes

Download as **PNG** for the widest compatibility or **WebP** for smaller files on websites. JPG cannot store transparency, so it is not offered here. To change the size afterwards, use the [Image Resizer](tool:image-resizer) and choose **PNG** under **Save as**. For online forms that want a small JPG signature on white, use the [signature resizer](tool:resize-signature) instead.

## Private and instant

## Step by step for a signature

1. Sign on white paper with a dark pen and photograph it from above.
2. Crop tightly around the signature.
3. Drop it here; the paper becomes transparent.
4. Raise **Tolerance** a little if grey patches remain.
5. Download as **PNG** and insert it into your document.

## Step by step for a logo

1. Start from the largest version of the logo you have.
2. Drop it here; if the background is not white, use **Pick from image**.
3. Check the inside of letters and shapes for areas that should stay.
4. Adjust **Edge softness** until the edges look smooth.
5. Download and test on a dark background.

## Common problems

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Light halo around the edges | Increase edge softness slightly |
| Parts of the design disappear | Lower the tolerance |
| Grey shadows remain | Raise the tolerance, or brighten the scan first |
| Background still visible | Make sure you picked the actual background colour |
| Result has a white box in another app | Download as PNG or WebP, not JPG |

## Why this is faster than AI for flat backgrounds

Colour-based removal looks at each pixel's colour, which is a simple calculation, so results appear instantly even for large scans. AI models analyse shapes and objects, which takes longer and is designed for photos. For paper, logos and solid backgrounds, the colour method is both faster and more precise.
