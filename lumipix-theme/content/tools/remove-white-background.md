Got a logo that came as a JPG with an ugly white box around it? Or a signature on paper you want to drop into a Word file? Here you can remove the white background from an image in one click and save a transparent PNG. No AI needed for this one, so it's instant, even with big scans, and nothing is uploaded.

## Instant transparency for flat backgrounds

When the background is plain white, like a scanned signature, a logo, a stamp or a drawing on paper, you don't need AI. This tool checks how close each pixel is to the background colour and fades it out smoothly, so the edges stay soft and natural.

## How to remove a white background

1. **Drop your image.** White areas turn transparent straight away.
2. **Check the preview.** The checkerboard on the right shows what's transparent.
3. **Raise Tolerance** if light grey bits are left, for example when the paper isn't pure white.
4. **Adjust Edge softness** to smooth the transition around lines and shapes.
5. **Download as PNG** (or WebP) to keep the transparency.

## Removing other colours

The background doesn't have to be white. Click **Pick from image**, then click the background in the original preview, and that colour is the one removed. You can also choose a colour with the colour picker. It works for logos on coloured boxes, evenly lit green screens and stamps on tinted paper. For logos and effects on black, there's a page to [remove black background from image](tool:remove-black-background) files.

## The two sliders

| Slider | What it does | Raise it when | Lower it when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tolerance | How different a colour can be from the background and still go | The paper looks grey or uneven | Bits of the design start vanishing |
| Edge softness | How gradually pixels near the edge fade | Edges look jagged | Edges look blurry or faint |

Start with the defaults, then nudge them in small steps while you watch the preview.

## No white halo

Simple tools often leave a pale fringe around the edges, because the edge pixels are a mix of ink and paper. This tool takes the background colour out of those pixels too, so a dark signature or logo looks clean on anything, dark mode and coloured slides included.

## Getting a scan ready

1. Scan or photograph the paper flat in even light.
2. Crop close around the content.
3. Brighten it a little so the paper is close to white.
4. Drop it here and fine-tune the sliders.

## Formats

Download as **PNG** if you want it to work everywhere, or **WebP** for smaller files on a website. JPG can't store transparency, so it isn't offered. To change the size afterwards, use the [image resizer](tool:image-resizer) and pick **PNG** under **Save as**. ## For a signature

1. Sign on white paper with a dark pen and take a photo from above.
2. Crop tight around the signature.
3. Drop it here. The paper turns transparent.
4. Raise **Tolerance** a little if grey patches are left.
5. Download as **PNG** and insert it into your document.

## For a logo

1. Start with the biggest version of the logo you have.
2. Drop it here. If the background isn't white, use **Pick from image**.
3. Check inside letters and shapes for areas that should stay.
4. Adjust **Edge softness** until the edges look smooth.
5. Download it and test it on a dark background.

If you're turning the logo into a site icon next, the [favicon to ICO](tool:favicon-generator) generator takes the transparent PNG as it is.

## Common problems

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| A light halo around the edges | Raise edge softness slightly |
| Parts of the design disappear | Lower the tolerance |
| Grey shadows are left | Raise the tolerance, or brighten the scan first |
| The background is still there | Check you picked the actual background colour |
| A white box shows up in another app | Download as PNG or WebP, not JPG |

## Why it's faster than AI here

Removing by colour just looks at each pixel's colour, which is a quick sum, so the result is instant even for large scans. AI models study shapes and objects, which takes longer and is meant for photos. For paper, logos and solid backgrounds, the colour method is quicker and more precise.
