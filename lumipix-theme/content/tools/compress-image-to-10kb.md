Ten kilobytes is about as strict as upload limits get. If a bank, college or job portal is asking for a signature under 10KB, you're in the right place. This page lets you compress an image to 10KB in your browser and keeps the result as clear as that tiny budget allows.

## Why 10KB needs a different approach

A normal phone photo is around 3MB. Getting it to 10KB means making it about three hundred times smaller. Lowering the quality alone can't do that without turning the picture into mush, so the image has to get smaller in pixels too.

That's already set up here. The target is 10KB and the maximum width starts at 400 pixels, which suits signatures and small portraits. Drop your file and the compressor finds the cleanest version that fits.

## How to compress an image to 10KB

1. **Crop it tightly** around the signature or the face first.
2. **Drop the image** on this page or tap **Choose files**.
3. **Download** once you see **Fits target**.

Need 5KB or 15KB? Tap one of the quick buttons, or type any number you like.

## Getting a sharp 10KB signature

Here's some good news: signatures are the easiest thing to fit into 10KB. They're mostly white paper with a few dark lines, and that compresses really well. To give yourself the best shot:

- Sign with a **bold black or dark blue pen**. A gel pen or felt-tip works better than a thin ballpoint.
- Use **plain white paper** with no lines.
- Take the photo **from straight above**, in daylight, with no shadow from your phone.
- **Crop close** to the ink and leave just a small margin.
- If the paper looks grey, **brighten** it a little before you compress.

## What about photos at 10KB?

A face can fit in 10KB, but only at a small size, usually 200 to 300 pixels wide. That's fine for forms that show a little thumbnail. To keep it clear:

| Do | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Plain, light background | Patterned walls and busy rooms |
| Head-and-shoulders crop | Full-body shots |
| Soft daylight on the face | Dark, grainy photos |
| The original camera file | Screenshots and forwarded copies |

If the form allows more, go for it. A photo at [resize image to 20KB](tool:compress-image-to-20kb) or [compress image to 50KB](tool:compress-image-to-50kb) will look noticeably sharper.

## When the form gives a minimum too

Some forms say something like "between 4KB and 10KB". If your result comes out under the minimum, open **Advanced options** and raise the **Maximum width**, to 600 for example. That keeps more detail and makes the file a bit bigger.

If the form lists exact pixel dimensions, set them first with the [image resizer](tool:image-resizer), then come back and compress with the maximum width left empty.

## Why a 10KB upload still gets rejected

- **It's a few bytes over.** Some portals count 1KB as 1,000 bytes instead of 1,024. Lumi Pix aims under the stricter number so you're covered.
- **Wrong format.** Upload the JPG you downloaded here, not the original HEIC or PNG.
- **Wrong dimensions.** Resize to the size the form asks for, then compress.
- **You picked the original.** On a phone, choose the file from Downloads or Files, not from your gallery.
