Use this tool to compress image to 10KB online for signatures, thumbnails and small ID photos, free and without uploading anything.

## The smallest common upload limit

Ten kilobytes is the tightest limit you are likely to meet. It appears most often for **signatures** on recruitment, admission and banking forms, and sometimes for tiny thumbnails or ID photos in older systems. A typical phone photo is 3MB, so a 10KB file is about three hundred times smaller.

At this size, quality alone cannot do the job. The image must also be small in pixels. This page is set up for that: the target is 10KB and the maximum width starts at 400 pixels, which suits signatures and small portraits. Drop your image and the compressor finds the clearest version that fits.

## How to compress to 10KB

1. **Crop tightly** around the signature or face before you start.
2. **Drop the image** on this page or tap **Choose files**.
3. **Download** when the result shows **Fits target**.

Need 5KB or 15KB instead? Use the quick buttons, or type any number in the custom field.

## Signatures: the ideal 10KB image

A signature is mostly white paper with a few dark lines, which compresses extremely well. To get a crisp 10KB signature:

- Sign with a **bold black or dark blue pen**, such as a gel or felt-tip.
- Use **plain white paper** without lines.
- Photograph it **from directly above** in daylight, without shadows.
- **Crop closely** around the ink, leaving a small margin.
- If the paper looks grey, **brighten** the photo slightly before compressing.

## Photos at 10KB

A portrait can fit in 10KB, but only at a small size, usually 200–300 pixels wide. That is enough for forms that show a small thumbnail. For a clearer photo:

| Do | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Plain, light background | Patterns and busy rooms |
| Head-and-shoulders crop | Full-body photos |
| Soft, even daylight | Dark photos with noise |
| Original camera file | Screenshots and forwarded copies |

If the form allows more, a [20KB](tool:compress-image-to-20kb) or [50KB](tool:compress-image-to-50kb) photo will look noticeably sharper.

## What happens inside the compressor

The tool first tries a high JPG quality at the width limit. If the file is still above 10KB, it searches for the highest quality that fits. If that quality would be too low to look acceptable, it reduces the width a little and tries again. The result is the sharpest file the limit allows, never a file that is a few bytes over.

## When there is a minimum as well

Some forms say "between 4KB and 10KB". If your result is below the minimum, open **Advanced options** and raise the **Maximum width**, for example to 600, so more detail is kept. If the form gives exact pixel dimensions, resize to them first with the [Image Resizer](tool:image-resizer), then compress here with the maximum width left empty.

## Common reasons a 10KB upload fails

- **The file is slightly over.** Some portals count 1KB as 1,000 bytes. Lumi Pix aims under that stricter number.
- **Wrong format.** Upload the downloaded JPG, not the original HEIC or PNG.
- **Wrong dimensions.** Resize to the stated size before compressing.
- **The original was uploaded by mistake.** On phones, pick the file from Downloads or Files, not the gallery.
