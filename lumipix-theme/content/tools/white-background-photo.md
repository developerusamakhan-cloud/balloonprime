## Tips for the cleanest result

- **Contrast helps.** If you stand out from the background, the AI gets clear edges.
- **Stay in focus.** Blurry edges are harder to separate.
- **Use even light.** Strong shadows on you can be mistaken for background.
- **Fill the frame.** If you're tiny in the photo, crop closer first.
- **Use the original photo**, not a screenshot of it.

## ID and passport photos

## White or transparent?

Choose **white and JPG** when the photo goes straight into a form, a listing or a profile. JPG files are smaller and accepted everywhere. Choose **transparent and PNG** when you'll place the subject on a design, a slide or a poster and want to pick the background later. You can switch between the two without processing the photo again.

## Making it look natural

A cut-out on pure white can look flat if the original light was very warm or very dark. Take the photo in soft daylight, avoid strongly coloured lamps, and keep the camera at eye level. If the edges around hair look rough, try light grey instead of pure white. It hides small imperfections and still counts as a plain background. For product photos, keep the camera height and distance the same for every item so the whole catalogue matches.
