## Which size does India use?

The size depends on where you apply and which office handles your form:

- **35 × 45 mm (3.5 × 4.5 cm)** is the size commonly asked for in applications made in India.
- **2 × 2 inches (51 × 51 mm)** is often asked for in applications made through Indian missions and their service centres in the United States.

Rules can change and some forms add their own requirements for head size, background and file size. Always read the latest instructions from the office that will receive your application before you print or upload.

## Tips for an accepted photo

- Use even light from the front. Avoid shadows on the face or the wall behind you.
- Ask someone else to take the photo from about 1.5 metres away. Close selfies distort the face.
- Remove glasses if the rules ask for it, and keep hair away from the eyes.
- Use the original photo from the camera, not one sent through a messaging app.
