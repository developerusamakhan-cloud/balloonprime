## Which size does India use?

It depends on where you're applying and which office handles your form:

- **35 x 45 mm (3.5 x 4.5 cm)** is the size usually asked for in applications made in India.
- **2 x 2 inches (51 x 51 mm)** is often asked for when you apply through Indian missions and their service centres in the United States.

Rules can change, and some forms add their own requirements for head size, background and file size. Always read the latest instructions from the office that will get your application before you print or upload.

## Tips for a photo that gets accepted

- Use even light from the front, with no shadows on your face or the wall.
- Ask someone else to take it from about 1.5 metres away. Close-up selfies distort your face.
- Take your glasses off if the rules ask for it, and keep hair away from your eyes.
- Use the original from your camera, not one that's been sent through WhatsApp.
