Most online background removers upload your photo to a server, process it there, and then offer a small free preview while charging for the full-resolution file. Lumi Pix works differently. ## Getting cleaner edges

- **Contrast helps.** A subject that stands out from the background gives the model clear edges to follow.
- **Keep the subject in focus.** Blurry edges are hard to separate.
- **Use a large original.** More pixels mean more edge detail.
- **Fill the frame.** If the subject is tiny in a big scene, crop closer before removing the background.

If a few hairs look rough, placing the cut-out on a background colour similar to the original usually hides it.

## PNG or JPG?

Choose **PNG** when you need transparency, for example to place the subject in a design. Choose **JPG** when you have picked a solid background, such as white for a listing or form; the file will be much smaller. A JPG cannot store transparency, so if you choose JPG with the transparent option, the background becomes white.

## Why it takes a moment the first time

The AI model is a few megabytes. Your browser downloads it once and stores it in its cache. After that, processing happens locally and quickly, and you can even remove backgrounds offline. Clearing your browser's site data removes the cached model, and it will download again next time.

## What happens to image quality

The result has the same resolution as your original photo. Edges are blended so the cut-out looks natural on new backgrounds. If you choose JPG, the image is saved at high quality with a solid background; PNG keeps the transparency without any compression loss.

## Tips for professional-looking cut-outs

- **Shoot against a plain wall** when you know you will remove the background later; it gives the cleanest edges.
- **Keep some space around the subject** so hair and shoulders are not cut off by the frame.
- **Match the light** of the new background: a cut-out with warm light looks odd on a cold blue background.
- **Add a subtle shadow** in a design app if the subject looks like it is floating.

## Limits to be aware of

AI background removal is impressive but not perfect. Transparent objects such as glass, very fine hair against a similar-coloured background, and subjects that blend into the scene can be difficult. For those, a manual edit in a desktop editor gives the most control.
