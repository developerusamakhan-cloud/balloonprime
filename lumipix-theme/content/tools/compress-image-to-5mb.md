## When you need an image under 5MB

A 5MB limit is common on job portals, school and university applications, support forms, marketplaces and some email services. Photos from modern cameras and phones, and high resolution scans, can easily be 8MB, 12MB or more. They need to shrink, but only a little.

## Will it look worse?

At 5MB, almost never in a way you can see. A 12 megapixel photo saved as a high quality JPG is usually around 3 to 6MB, so lots of photos only need a light touch. Really big images, like panoramas or 50 megapixel camera files, may also be scaled down a little to fit. Even then they keep far more pixels than any screen or normal print needs.

Planning to print it big? Keep the original too, and only upload the compressed copy where the limit applies.

## PNG, WebP and screenshots

PNG files are lossless, which means they can be enormous. The compressor saves its results as JPG, and that usually brings a PNG photo or screenshot well under 5MB straight away. Transparent areas turn white in a JPG, though. For a logo that has to stay transparent, keep it as a PNG and make it smaller with the [image resizer](tool:image-resizer) instead.
