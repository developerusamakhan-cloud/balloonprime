## Printing from a 2MB file

A 12 megapixel photo is about 4000 x 3000 pixels. At 2MB it usually keeps that resolution, which prints sharp up to about 13 x 10 inches at 300 DPI. If you need an exact print size, first [resize image into inches](tool:resize-image-in-cm) or centimetres, then compress here. The DPI value in a JPG is kept, so the print size stays right.

## Do you even need 2MB?

If the photo is only going on a screen, 2MB is more than enough. For websites, 200KB to 500KB per image loads much faster. For email, 1MB per photo is usually plenty. Try [compress image to 1MB](tool:compress-image-to-1mb) or [compress image to 500KB](tool:compress-image-to-500kb) instead.

## Really big photos

Phones with 48 or 50 megapixel cameras make huge files. If even a lower quality can't reach 2MB, the compressor shrinks the dimensions a little at a time. ## Tips for the best result

- **Start from the original**, not a copy that went through a chat app.
- **Don't save photos as PNG.** JPG is far better for photographs.
- **Crop out what you don't need**, especially in panoramas.
- **Keep your originals** somewhere safe. Compression can't be undone.
