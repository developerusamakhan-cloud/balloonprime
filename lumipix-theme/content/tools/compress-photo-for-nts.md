## Preparing your photo for NTS registration

The National Testing Service (NTS) conducts tests in Pakistan for admissions, scholarships and recruitment on behalf of many organisations, including tests such as NAT and GAT. Online registration usually requires a photograph, and some tests also ask for other documents. The photo must meet the size and format rules stated in the instructions for your test.

This page helps you get your photo ready in a minute. Choose the limit, drop your photo, and download a JPG that fits. It all runs in your browser, so your photo is never uploaded to Lumi Pix.

**Important:** NTS conducts tests for different organisations, and requirements can vary between tests. Always follow the instructions for your specific test. Lumi Pix is an independent tool and is not affiliated with NTS.

## Step by step

1. **Check the test instructions** for the photo size limit, format and any dimensions.
2. **Take a recent photo** facing the camera, against a plain light background.
3. **Crop** to head and shoulders, or to exact proportions with the [passport size photo maker](tool:passport-size-photo).
4. **Pick the limit** on this page using the quick buttons or custom size.
5. **Drop the photo** and wait for **Fits target**.
6. **Download** the JPG and upload it during registration.

## Why one test may differ from another

Because NTS runs tests for universities, scholarship programmes and employers, each test can come with its own registration form and rules. A photo that worked for one test may not fit the next. Keep your original photo and re-compress it whenever the limit changes. That gives a sharper result than compressing an already small file again.

## Getting a good photo at home

| Do | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Plain white or light wall | Patterned curtains or posters |
| Daylight from the front | Overhead lights casting shadows |
| Camera at eye level, about a metre away | Selfies at arm's length |
| Neutral expression | Filters and beauty modes |
| Recent photo | Old photos that look different |

If your background is not plain, use the [Background Remover](tool:background-remover) with a white background, then crop and compress.

## Other files you might need

Some registrations also ask for a signature, an identity document or academic records. Prepare them the same way:

- **Signature:** sign on white paper, crop tightly, use the [signature resizer](tool:resize-signature).
- **ID card or B-form:** scan flat, crop, and compress, for example with [compress to 200KB](tool:compress-image-to-200kb).
- **Results and certificates:** scan clearly and check that grades and dates are readable.

## Registering from a phone

Most students register from a phone. Open this page in your mobile browser, tap **Choose files**, select the cropped photo and download the result. It is saved in **Downloads** on Android or the **Files** app on iPhone. Pick it from there in the registration form.

## Troubleshooting

- **Rejected for size:** choose a slightly smaller limit, because some systems count 1KB as 1,000 bytes.
- **Rejected for format:** upload the downloaded JPG; iPhone HEIC photos are converted automatically here.
- **Rejected for dimensions:** resize to the stated pixels with the [Image Resizer](tool:image-resizer) first.
- **Photo looks blurry:** crop tighter and use a plainer background so more detail fits within the limit.

## Private and free

Your photo is processed on your own device. Nothing is uploaded, stored or shared, and there is no account or watermark.

## A simple routine before every registration

| Step | What to do |
|---|---|
| 1 | Read the test announcement and note the photo requirements |
| 2 | Use your saved original photo, or take a new one if it is old |
| 3 | Crop and compress to the stated limit on this page |
| 4 | Check the face is clear and the file is a JPG |
| 5 | Register early, before the last-day rush |

## Students applying for several tests

Many students register for more than one test in a year, for admissions and scholarships. Keep a single high-quality original photo and create a new compressed copy for each registration, matched to that test's limit. Name the copies clearly, for example `photo-nat.jpg` or `photo-gat.jpg`, so you upload the right one.

## Admit cards and printing

Photos uploaded during registration may appear on admit cards and result documents. A clear, well-lit photo against a plain background looks better when printed small. Avoid photos with sunglasses, caps, heavy shadows or busy backgrounds.

## Quick sizes on this page

The quick buttons cover 20KB, 50KB, 100KB and 200KB. If your test states another limit, type it into the custom field. If it specifies exact dimensions, use the Image Resizer first and then compress the result here.
