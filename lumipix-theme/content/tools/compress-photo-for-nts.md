## Getting your photo ready for NTS

The National Testing Service runs tests in Pakistan for admissions, scholarships and jobs on behalf of lots of organisations. Online registration nearly always asks for a photograph, and some tests ask for other documents too. The photo has to match the size and format rules for your test.

Choose the limit, drop your photo and download the result.

**Please note:** NTS runs tests for many different organisations, so the rules can change from test to test. Always follow the instructions for your specific test. Lumi Pix is an independent tool and isn't connected to NTS.

## Step by step

1. **Check your test's instructions** for the photo limit, format and any dimensions.
2. **Take a recent photo** facing the camera, against a plain light background.
3. **Crop it** to head and shoulders, or to an exact shape with the [passport size photo creator](tool:passport-size-photo).
4. **Pick the limit** with the quick buttons or type your own.
5. **Drop the photo** and wait for **Fits target**.
6. **Download the JPG** and upload it while you register.

## Why the rules change between tests

NTS tests are run for universities, scholarship programmes and employers, and each can come with its own form and rules. A photo that worked last time might not fit the next one. Keep your original photo and compress it again whenever the limit changes. You'll get a sharper result than squeezing an already small file again.

## A good photo at home

| Do | Avoid |

## Registering on your phone

Most students register from a phone, and that's fine. Open this page in your browser, tap **Choose files**, pick the cropped photo and download the result. It's saved in **Downloads** on Android or the **Files** app on iPhone. Pick it from there in the registration form.

## If it gets rejected

- **Too big.** Choose a slightly smaller limit, because some systems count 1KB as 1,000 bytes.
- **Wrong format.** Upload the JPG you downloaded. iPhone HEIC photos are converted here automatically.
- **Wrong dimensions.** Set the size in pixels in the [image resizer](tool:image-resizer) first.
- **Blurry.** Crop tighter and use a plainer background so more detail fits.

## Taking several tests this year?

Lots of students register for more than one test a year. Keep one good original photo and make a fresh copy for each registration at that test's limit. Name the copies clearly, like `photo-nat.jpg` or `photo-gat.jpg`, so you upload the right one.

## Admit cards and printing

The photo you upload may end up on your admit card and result documents. A clear, well lit photo against a plain background looks much better printed small. Leave out sunglasses, caps, heavy shadows and busy backgrounds.
