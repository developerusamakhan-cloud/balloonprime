Compression is permanent. A 1MB copy is perfect for screens, but for big prints you'll want the original. Keep your originals in a separate folder and only compress copies. ## JPG or WebP?

JPG is the safe choice for email and uploads. If the images are for your own website, choose **WebP** under **Save as**, since it's usually smaller at the same quality. Just check the site or app accepts WebP before you switch.

## A note on screenshots

Screenshots are often saved as PNG and can be several megabytes. Turning them into a JPG under 1MB works well for photos and app screens. If the screenshot is full of small text, zoom to 100% and check it. If the text looks soft, keep the PNG or use a higher limit.
