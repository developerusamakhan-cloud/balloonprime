## Why 20KB is tricky

A typical phone photo is somewhere between 2MB and 5MB. To get under 20KB it has to shrink by one or two hundred times. Recruitment portals, admission forms and some government services still use this limit, because they show the images small and store thousands of them.

Squeezing quality alone won't get you there. The picture turns into coloured blocks. What works is making the image smaller in pixels as well, so a decent quality fits inside the budget. This page does that for you. The target is 20KB and the maximum width starts at 600 pixels, which suits both signatures and portraits.

## How to resize an image to 20KB

1. **Crop first.** Use your phone's gallery to crop a portrait to head and shoulders, or a signature close around the ink.

## How to get a clearer 20KB photo

- **Stand in front of a plain, light wall.** The face gets more of the file that way.
- **Use even light.** Dark photos are grainy, and grain uses up kilobytes fast.
- **Start from the camera original.** Screenshots and WhatsApp copies have already lost detail.
- **Don't compress the same file twice.** Each round removes a little more. Go back to the original instead.

## When there's a minimum size as well

Some forms say "between 10KB and 20KB". If your result lands under the minimum because the image is very simple, open **Advanced options** and raise the **Maximum width**, to 800 for example. More pixels means a slightly bigger file. If the form lists exact pixel dimensions, set those first in the [image resizer](tool:image-resizer).

## Common reasons the form says no

- **Wrong format.** The form wants JPG. This tool always saves JPG, so make sure you upload the downloaded file.
- **Just over the limit.** Some portals count 1KB as 1,000 bytes, others as 1,024. Lumi Pix aims under the stricter one.
- **Wrong dimensions.** The file size is fine but the width and height aren't. Resize to what the form asks for first.
- **You picked the original.** On a phone, choose the file from Downloads or Files, not the gallery.
