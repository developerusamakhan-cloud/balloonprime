## Canada passport photo size

Canadian passport photos are **50 mm wide and 70 mm high**. That's larger than the 35 x 45 mm size many other countries use. The Government of Canada also has rules for head size, the background, lighting and the details printed on the back of the photo. Read the current official photo specifications before you print, because they decide whether your photo is accepted.

## Tips for a passport photo at home

- Stand about a metre from the wall so you don't cast a shadow on it.
- Face a window in daylight for soft, even light.
- Ask someone else to take the photo from about 1.5 metres away, with the camera at eye level.
- Keep a neutral face, mouth closed and both eyes open.
- Use the original from your camera, not a copy sent through a chat app.
