## Canada passport photo size

Canadian passport photos are **50 mm wide and 70 mm high**, which is larger than the 35 × 45 mm size used by many other countries. The Government of Canada also sets rules for the head size, the background, lighting and the information printed on the back of the photo. Read the current official photo specifications before you print, because they decide whether the photo is accepted.

## Tips for a good passport photo at home

- Stand about a metre from the wall so you do not cast a shadow on it.
- Face a window in daylight for soft, even light on the face.
- Ask someone else to take the photo from about 1.5 metres away, with the camera at eye level.
- Keep a neutral expression with your mouth closed and both eyes open.
- Use the original photo from your camera, not a copy sent through a messaging app.
